## What Mari is

Mari is a free anti-scam moderation bot. It checks links and images posted in
messages, removes scams, and can ban, time out or warn the people who post
them. It also translates messages when someone reacts with a country flag.

## Server administrators

- You are responsible for the actions Mari takes in your server under that
  configuration, and for telling your members that Mari moderates the server.
- Mari's decisions can be reviewed: every automatic ban carries a
  *Wrong - unban* button, and unclear cases are sent to moderators instead of
  being punished. Please review them.
